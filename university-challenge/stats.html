Most-asked subjects

    Composers

    Music questions naming each composer in the clue or accepted answer, plus music rounds where a recording was played and the accepted answer names the composer. Open a name to see examples.

    Operas and musicals

    Titles given as the accepted answer when a question or a played excerpt asks for the opera or musical.

    Bands and artists

    Performers given as the accepted answer when a question or a played recording asks for the band, singer or artist.

        Asked again

        The same or a very similar question in more than one broadcast: the same accepted answer reached through closely matching clues. Some are a recurring format rather than a word-for-word repeat. Open one to see each time it came up.

        Best blind guesses

        The answer that comes up most often in each category, and your odds of being right if you gave it to every question in that category without hearing them. Choose a category to see its top answers.
