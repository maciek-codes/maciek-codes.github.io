“Nominate…”

On a bonus, the captain names who should answer. These captains said “nominate” most often during their own team's bonuses. Older series use automatic captions, which often miss it, so most of the leaders are from Series 53 onwards.

Series 49 to 52 use YouTube's automatic captions and later competitions use broadcast subtitles. Automatic captions can miss short interjections, so differences of a few per broadcast between the two hosts may partly reflect the captions. Bigger differences, like “ten points for this” against “another starter question”, are in how they present.
