The canon

How far a list gets you

The bar is the share of each subject's questions answered by its 100 most frequent answers. The black tick is the share that falls in its 100 biggest themes, such as English monarchs or the planets. Choose a subject to open it.

    Would past papers have helped?

    For each series, the share of its answers that had already come up in an earlier broadcast in the bank. Darker is the share that had come up three times or more.

      The bank holds more earlier questions for later series, which is part of why the share rises.

        Books to read

          Themes group questions that are about the same kind of thing, found by comparing the meaning of every question and its answer on a computer, so some groupings are rough and a theme's name is just its commonest answers. Answers are compared after dropping brackets, alternatives, articles and accents, so a few spellings of one answer may still be counted apart. Counts are of accepted answers, not clues: the same answer is often reached through different facts.
